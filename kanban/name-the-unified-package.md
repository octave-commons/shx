# Name the combined concept

## Constraint

Must sit in the existing Greek lattice: Clio (history), Axxium (identity),
Keryx (herald), Nomos (internal law), Psephisma (decree with external
consequence), Rheos (flow).

User has stated they do not love "shx" but it is "probably fine".

## Proposal: Hexis

Aristotle: ethos (repeated practice) crystallizes into hexis (the settled
disposition you then act from). Homer's ethos is the accustomed haunt, the
dwelling place of an animal. One word covers shell, habit, and character.

The naming IS the mechanism:

| Aristotle | event sourcing | this system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ethos, repeated acts | the append-only ledger | resources, receipts |
| hexis, settled disposition | the projection | CLAUDE.md, PATH, opencode.json |

Anchored in the user's own phrasing: "how much my habit fields have been
shaping around the existence of these terminals."

Keeps the x from shx.

## Runner-up

Ethos, leaning on the dwelling-place sense. Commoner word, sits less well
beside Psephisma.
