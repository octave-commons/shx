## Assistant

The bubbling problem is the real tell: bash's `source` is *order-dependent side effects in a process* — a sourced file that sources another file is at the mercy of subshells, export discipline, and who-evaluated-whom. EDN fragments don't have that problem, because merging maps is not a process. The fix is to make composition **data**: a fragment that includes a fragment is just a tree-fold, and everything lands in the final render. Nothing to bubble.

(The bigger dream has honorable ancestors — scsh, eshell, closh, nushell. The honest architecture is two-layer: bash stays the engine for other people's work, Clojure becomes the brain that *renders* it. `render bash` being a target leaves room for `render closh` later.)
