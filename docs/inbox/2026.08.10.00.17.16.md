## Qualification addendum — 2026-10-03

The seven lines above are the original incomplete user capture, not a finished
runtime contract. Derived vocabulary for the proposed actor model:

- **Nomos**: an actor's decision protocol over admitted messages/history.
- **Psephisma**: an authoritative, addressable decision produced by that protocol,
  represented as data; consequential requests can be enacted outside memory.
- **Praxis**: enactment of an admitted decision by an effectful adapter.

The unfinished `stores` and `Praxis 36` fragments are preserved as source, not
normative definitions. Complete identity/admission/effect laws still require the
reviewed actor specification; no runtime semantics are invented by these names.
See ADR-0002/0003 and RP-001 for prerequisites.
